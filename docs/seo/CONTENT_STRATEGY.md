## 1. Editorial Philosophy & Standards

To build sustainable, long-term search visibility and avoid Google Helpful Content / Core Algorithm penalties, Accero PrintPortal enforces strict editorial guidelines:

1. **Grounded Practical Utility:** Every article must address a real operational question faced by an operator working behind a physical printing counter in India (e.g. printer tray margin cutoffs, Devanagari font rendering, missing demographic fields, customer reprint policies).
2. **Anti-Filler & Anti-Slop:** No mass-produced generic AI filler. Articles must feature specific hardware models (Epson L805, L850, L8050, Canon Pixma G3010), exact physical dimensions (85.60 mm × 53.98 mm, 3.18 mm radius), and actual workflow steps.
3. **Natural Conversion Bridging:** Contextual links point naturally to free utilities (e.g., [CR80 Card Size Calculator](/tools/cr80-card-size-calculator)), specialized portal pages ([CSC Print Portal](/csc-print-portal), [Cyber Cafe Software](/cyber-cafe-printing-software)), and live document canvases ([PAN Card Printing](/services/pan-card-printing)).
4. **Strict Legal & Compliance Safeguards:** Never claim official government authority. Never promote illegal card manufacturing. Always emphasize legitimate document formatting with explicit citizen consent.

### Contextual Anchor Text Rules:
* Use descriptive semantic phrases: `"explore our CSC print portal"`, `"calculate CR80 card dimensions in mm"`, `"format PAN card on PVC tray"`.
* Avoid generic anchors such as `"click here"`, `"read more"`, or repetitive exact-match spam.
