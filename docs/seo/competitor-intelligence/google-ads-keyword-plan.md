## 1. Google Ads Policy & Trademark Compliance Rules

Before allocating budget to paid search, Accero must comply with Google's Trademark and Misrepresentation policies:

1. **Competitor Keywords in Bidding:** Google Ads permits bidding on competitor trademark keywords as search triggers (Exact match `[competitor]` or Phrase match `"competitor alternative"`).
2. **Competitor Trademarks in Ad Copy:** Under Google's Trademarks Policy (India region), advertisers **must not use competitor trademarks in the visible ad text or display URL** in a manner that confuses consumers regarding sponsorship, affiliation, or origin.
   - *Prohibited Ad Copy:* "Official Digital Portal Login - Click Here" (Misleading/Deceptive).
   - *Permitted Comparative Ad Copy:* "Looking for a More Reliable Print Portal? Try Accero. Zero Ruined PVC Cards & Free Lifetime Reprints."
3. **Landing Page Experience:** The landing page must provide genuine, non-deceptive, substantial comparison information and clearly state that Accero is an independent software tool.
