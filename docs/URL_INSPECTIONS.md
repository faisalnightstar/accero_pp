## ⚡ How to Inspect & Request Indexing

1. Click on any **[Inspect in GSC]** direct link below (or copy the URL and paste it into the top search bar of Google Search Console).
2. Wait for Google to retrieve data from the index.
3. Click **"Test Live URL"** (top right) to confirm Googlebot can successfully fetch the page without rendering blocks.
4. Click **"Request Indexing"**.

> ⚠️ **Google Quota Advisory:**  
> Google Search Console enforces an unpublished daily quota of approximately **10 to 15 manual "Request Indexing" submissions per property per 24 hours**.  
> If you attempt to submit all 41 URLs at once, Google will return a *"Quota exceeded"* message.

* ❌ `/dashboard/*` (Operator dashboard, transaction histories, wallet ledgers)
* ❌ `/studio` & `/studio/*` (Card editing canvas & dynamic preview)
* ❌ `/wallet` & `/wallet/*` (Payment top-ups and balances)
* ❌ `/cards` & `/cards/*` (Saved print cards and user document cache)
* ❌ `/admin/*` (Superadmin console & finance controls)
* ❌ `/support/*` (Operator support tickets and sessions)
* ❌ `/login` (Auth gateway — strictly meta `noindex`)
* ❌ `/register` (Registration gateway — strictly meta `noindex`)
* ❌ `/forgot-password` & `/reset-password` (Strictly meta `noindex`)
* ❌ `/api/*` (Backend API routes)

## 🔎 Verifying Crawl & Indexation Success in GSC

After submitting each wave:
1. **Pages Report:** Navigate to `Indexing > Pages`. Check the "Indexed" count increases over 3 to 7 days.
2. **Sitemaps Tab:** Confirm `sitemap.xml` shows status **Success** with **41 discovered URLs**.
3. **URL Inspection Verdict:** A healthy crawled page will show:
   * **Presence:** *"URL is on Google"* (after crawl)
   * **Coverage:** *"Submitted and indexed"*
   * **Page fetch:** *"Successful"*
   * **Indexing allowed?:** *"Yes"*
   * **User-declared canonical:** Matches Google-selected canonical (`https://printportal.accero.app/...`).
