## 1. Executive Strategy & Ethical Quality Gate

Accero PrintPortal operates under a **Strict White-Hat Backlink Policy**. 

### Prohibited Practices (Zero-Tolerance)
* **No Purchased Links:** Zero paid guest post placements on sponsored blog networks.
* **No Private Blog Networks (PBNs):** Zero expired domain manipulation or fake network creation.
* **No Manufactured Reviews or Testimonials:** Zero fake customer reviews submitted to directories or social media.
* **No Automated Spam:** Zero forum profile creation tools, blog comment spam, or mass directory submission software.
* **No Disavow Paranoia:** Google’s Penguin and SpamBrain algorithms automatically ignore low-quality scraper links. We do **not** disavow links simply because they look suspicious unless an explicit manual action is received in GSC.

### The Organic Link Philosophy
Backlinks in the Indian SME printing space must be **earned as voluntary citations** by providing free, indispensable technical utilities and industry benchmarking data that operators, developers, and trade journalists naturally reference.

## 3. Targeted Outreach Verticals & Channel Strategy

### Channel 1: B2B Software Directories (Legitimate Profiles Only)
* **Target Platforms:**
  - **TechJockey India** (India’s top software marketplace for retail & cyber cafe software).
  - **SoftwareSuggest** (Enterprise & SME software directory with a dedicated Printing Management category).
  - **G2 India & Capterra India** (Authoritative, verified user review platforms).
* **Execution Protocol:**
  - Claim official vendor profiles under legal entity name (*Accero Technologies*).
  - Provide complete technical documentation, system requirements, and canonical link to `/`.
  - **Do NOT manufacture fake reviews.** Collect authentic feedback only from verified active operators.

### Channel 2: Printing Technology Trade Publications
* **Target Publications:** *PrintWeek India*, *Screen Print India*, All India Photo Trade Association newsletters.
* **Story Pitch:** *"How Desktop EcoTank Inkjet Trays Are Replacing Heavy Thermal Card Printers Across Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian Counters."*
* **Angle:** Focus on hardware economics, ink yield comparisons, and zero-margin browser-native print technology.

### Channel 3: CSC & VLE Community Resource Sharing
* **Target Channels:** VLE Society forums, State CSC VLE Facebook groups, YouTube tech creators specializing in CSC tutorials.
* **Value Offering:** Provide Asset 1 (CR80 DPI cheat-sheet) and Asset 2 (Counter rate card PDF) as free, un-gated downloads.
* **Outreach Tone:** Strictly educational and helpful; zero aggressive sales pitching.

### Channel 4: Indian Startup & Tech Policy Publications

* **Story Pitch:** *"The Unseen Tech Stack Behind India's 500,000 Rural Digital Service Centers: How Browser-Native Architecture Replaces Insecure Legacy Software."*

## 5. Risk Management & Disavow Policy

* **Spam Scraper Links:** Third-party bot scraper domains (e.g. coupon aggregators or rank trackers) that scrape Accero URLs will be observed but **not disavowed**. Google's machine learning systems natively discount these without manual intervention.
* **Manual Review Trigger:** A formal Google Disavow file will be compiled and uploaded via GSC *only* if an official Manual Action penalty is issued by Google's webspam team.
