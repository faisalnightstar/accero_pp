## 1. Executive Strategy & Ethical Policy

To ensure sustainable domain authority growth that withstands Google algorithmic updates (Helpful Content and Spam Updates), Accero PrintPortal strictly rejects:
* Paid link schemes, guest post link farms, and private blog networks (PBNs).
* Automated comment spam, forum profile spam, or mass web 2.0 submissions.
* Reciprocal link-exchange rings ("you link to me, I link to you").

### The Core Authority Thesis
In the Indian cyber cafe and Common Service Centre (CSC) space, authoritative backlinks are naturally earned by creating **indispensable utility assets**: free technical calibration tools, cheat-sheets, printable rate cards, and authoritative legal compliance guidance that village entrepreneurs and hardware communities genuinely bookmark and cite.

## 2. High-Value Linkable Assets ("Link Magnets")

Accero PrintPortal will produce four primary linkable assets specifically engineered to attract editorial citations and community backlinks:

### Asset 1: The Interactive CR80 & PVC Tray Calibration Cheat-Sheet
* **Target URL:** [`/resources`](https://printportal.accero.app/resources) & [`/blog/cr80-card-dimensions-guide`](https://printportal.accero.app/blog/cr80-card-dimensions-guide)
* **What it is:** A web-based interactive tool and downloadable PDF reference card detailing exact millimetre, inch, point, and pixel dimensions (at 300 DPI, 600 DPI, and 1200 DPI) for ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard plastic cards.
* **Why it earns links:** Hardware discussion boards, graphic design forums, and YouTube tutorial creators constantly need an authoritative, non-spam reference link to explain card dimensions and printer margins to their audiences.

### Asset 2: Downloadable Cyber Cafe & CSC Counter Rate Card (Printable PDF)
* **Target URL:** [`/blog/cyber-cafe-document-printing-charges-india`](https://printportal.accero.app/blog/cyber-cafe-document-printing-charges-india)
* **What it is:** A professionally designed, high-contrast A4 printable rate card template that cyber cafe owners can print, laminate, and hang behind their service counters displaying market-rate benchmarks for photocopy, color printouts, lamination, and PVC smart cards.
* **Why it earns links:** VLE discussion portals, Facebook operator groups, and local digital trade blogs share and link directly to the download page.

### Asset 3: The DPDPA 2023 Compliance Guide for CSC Operators & Cyber Cafes
* **Target URL:** [`/security`](https://printportal.accero.app/security)
* **What it is:** A plain-language legal advisory explaining how India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 applies to local shops scanning citizen Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, and ration cards.
* **Why it earns links:** Legal tech blogs, SME advisory portals, and citizen governance researchers cite it as a practical case study in ephemeral data handling.

### Asset 4: Multi-Printer Driver Calibration Code Snippet Box
* **Target URL:** [`/blog/pvc-card-printer-tray-guide`](https://printportal.accero.app/blog/pvc-card-printer-tray-guide)
* **What it is:** Ready-to-copy CSS `@page { size: 85.6mm 53.98mm; margin: 0; }` snippets and driver margin coordinate tables for Epson L805, L8050, Canon G570, and G670 printers.
* **Why it earns links:** Cited as the go-to technical fix by developers, forum moderators, and printing shop hobbyists.

## 5. Link Velocity & Risk Mitigation Rules

1. **Velocity Benchmark:** Target acquiring **3 to 5 high-quality editorial links per month** rather than dozens of low-quality links.
2. **Anchor Text Diversity:**
   - 60% Brand & URL anchors: `Accero PrintPortal`, `printportal.accero.app`, `Accero`.
   - 25% Natural & Topical anchors: `CR80 dimensions reference`, `PVC card printer tray guide`, `print portal software`.
   - 15% Exact-match commercial anchors: `best print portal for csc`, `pvc card printing software`.
3. **Continuous Toxic Link Monitoring:** Monthly review of referring domains in Google Search Console to disavow spam scrapers immediately if detected.
