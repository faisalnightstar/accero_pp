## 3. CONTENT GAP EXECUTION & ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY

1. **Competitors are completely absent from Google Answer Engines (AEO):** None of the 5 competitors implement Schema.org `FAQPage`, `HowTo`, or `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD data. Accero implements full schema markup on every public route, allowing Google to pull direct answers into Featured Snippets and AI Overviews.
2. **Competitors fail on Generative Engine Discovery (GEO):** None of the 5 competitors provide `/llms.txt` or structured entity definitions. When users prompt ChatGPT Search or Perplexity, the models cannot find machine-readable specifications for legacy portals. Accero maintains complete `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` manifests.
3. **Accero bridges informational queries to commercial conversion:** By answering technical printer setup questions (`epson l805 tray j settings`, `cr80 card size in mm`), Accero attracts operators at the exact moment they are configuring their hardware, seamlessly introducing them to the cloud studio.
