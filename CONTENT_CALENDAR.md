## 2. PRODUCTION QUALITY CHECKLIST (PER ARTICLE)

Every article published under this strategy must satisfy all of the following requirements before going live:
1. **Word Count:** 1,200 to 2,000 words of original, expert-reviewed analysis.
2. **AEO Structure:** Includes at least one prominent H2 question with an immediate 40-60 word bold answer block.
3. **Structured Data:** Backed by Schema.org `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` JSON-LD markup.
4. **Original Data & Tables:** Contains at least one comparison table or hardware specification breakdown.
5. **Internal Linking:** Includes 3-5 contextual links pointing to related `/features/*`, `/services/*`, or `/solutions/*` pages.
6. **No Fake Claims:** Zero unsupported market leadership claims, zero fabricated customer testimonials, and zero false government affiliations.
